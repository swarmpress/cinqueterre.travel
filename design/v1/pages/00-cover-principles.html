Principles
i.Write like a dispatchEvery page is datelined, signed and timestamped. Mono metadata — place, time, read length — frames serif storytelling.
ii.Pigment is wayfindingEach village owns one facade pigment. It marks tags, menus and section rules — never decoration for its own sake.
iii.Motion follows the tideLong ease-out curves, small distances, one orchestrated reveal per view. Nothing bounces except a saved bookmark.
iv.Photographs carry the colourUI stays in salt-white and night-sea ink so imagery does the shouting. Square corners; images bleed, crop hard, sit in asymmetric grids.
Observed on the live site → fix
01Ratings mix ★, /5 and /10 across stay cards → one scale: /10, one decimal.
02H1s repeat the SEO title ("… | Cinque Terre Dispatch") → H1 is the headline only; brand lives in the masthead.
03Several CTAs point to # ("Where to Stay", "Trail Conditions") → every button needs a real target or is removed.
04Same Unsplash image reused across hero, cards and articles → one image per story; village pigment placeholder until shot.
05Trail stats are inline prose → the Trail Ledger component (distance, time, grade, card).
06"Uncategorized" posts and missing alt text → required category and alt fields in the CMS.
