Rules
  • Headlines are sentence case. Italic Bodoni marks one emphasised word per headline, never a whole line.
  • Body never set in Bodoni below 24px — hairlines vanish.
  • Eyebrows and meta in Mono uppercase only for ≤4 words; datelines stay mixed case.
  • Prices, temperatures, times: font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums.
  • Drop cap on the first paragraph of long-form only; Corallo, 3 lines deep.
  • text-wrap: balance on headings, pretty on paragraphs.
  • Italian place names keep accents and apostrophes: Ca' D'Andrean, Madüneta.
